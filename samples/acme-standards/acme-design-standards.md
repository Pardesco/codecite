# Acme Engineering Design Standards

Internal design standards for Acme Engineering projects. Where these standards are stricter than the adopted building code, these standards govern Acme deliverables.

## 1 General

### 1.1 Purpose

These standards set the minimum design criteria that Acme applies to every project regardless of jurisdiction. They are internal and do not replace the adopted code.

### 1.2 Precedence

Where a standard conflicts with the adopted code, the stricter requirement applies. Deviations require written approval from the discipline lead.

## 2 Definitions

CORRIDOR. An enclosed path of travel between rooms that Acme designs at not less than 60 inches clear regardless of occupant load.

EGRESS STAIR. A stair that forms part of a required exit, designed to the Acme stair standard in Section 4.

## 3 Egress Design

### 3.1 Corridor width

Acme designs corridors serving more than 10 occupants at not less than 60 inches clear width, and corridors in healthcare projects at not less than 96 inches. This exceeds the minimum corridor width in the adopted code.

### 3.2 Dead-end corridors

Acme limits dead-end corridors to 20 feet in all occupancies, sprinklered or not. The sprinklered dead-end allowance in the adopted code is not used on Acme projects.

### 3.3 Travel distance

Acme designs to 80 percent of the maximum exit access travel distance permitted by the adopted code, to leave margin for tenant fit-out changes.

## 4 Stairs

### 4.1 Risers and treads

Acme egress stairs use a 7-inch maximum riser and an 11-inch minimum tread in every occupancy, including within dwelling units. Spiral stairs are not used as egress components.

### 4.2 Handrails

Handrails are provided on both sides of every Acme egress stair at 36 inches above nosings, with a circular cross section of 1-1/2 inches outside diameter.

## 5 Fire Protection

### 5.1 Sprinklers

Every Acme building over 5,000 square feet is designed with an automatic sprinkler system throughout, whether or not the adopted code requires one.

### 5.2 Fire alarm

Every Acme building with an occupant load of 100 or more is designed with a fire alarm system with voice evacuation.
