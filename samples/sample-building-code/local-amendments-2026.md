# CHAPTER 1 SCOPE AND ADMINISTRATION

### 105.3 Expiration

Every permit expires if the authorised work is not commenced within 365 days after issuance, or if the work is suspended for a period of 365 days after it has commenced. The building official may grant up to two extensions of not more than 180 days each on written request accompanied by the extension fee.

# CHAPTER 5 BUILDING HEIGHTS AND AREAS

### 505.2 Area limitation

The aggregate area of mezzanines within a room shall not exceed one-third of the floor area of that room. Within the model jurisdiction, the enclosed portion of a room shall be included in the room area used to determine the maximum mezzanine area where the enclosed portion is sprinklered.

Exception: In Type I or Type II construction with an automatic sprinkler system throughout, the aggregate mezzanine area may be increased to one-half of the floor area of the room regardless of the number of storeys.

# CHAPTER 9 FIRE PROTECTION SYSTEMS

### 903.2.8 Group R

An automatic sprinkler system shall be provided throughout all buildings with a Group R fire area. The Model Residential Code exception is not adopted within the model jurisdiction; Group R-3 buildings with two or more dwelling units shall be sprinklered.

# CHAPTER 10 MEANS OF EGRESS

### 1004.5 Areas without fixed seating

The number of occupants shall be computed at the rate of one occupant per unit of area as prescribed in Table 1004.5, except that the occupant load factor for business areas shall be 100 gross square feet per occupant within the model jurisdiction.

### 1011.5 Stair treads and risers

Stair riser heights shall be 7 inches maximum and 4 inches minimum. Rectangular tread depths shall be 11 inches minimum. Within dwelling units in Group R-2 and R-3, the maximum riser height is 8 inches and the minimum tread depth is 9 inches. The tolerance between the largest and smallest riser height within any flight shall not exceed 3/8 inch.

### 1020.4 Dead ends

Where more than one exit or exit access doorway is required, the exit access shall be arranged such that there are no dead ends in corridors more than 20 feet in length.

Exception: In occupancies in Groups B, M, and S where the building is equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system in accordance with Section 903.3.1.1, the length of the dead-end corridors shall not exceed 35 feet.
