# CHAPTER 1 SCOPE AND ADMINISTRATION

### 105.3 Expiration

A permit lapses if the work has not started within 365 days of issuance, or if work stops for 365 days once it has started. On written request accompanied by the extension fee, the building official may grant up to two extensions of 180 days each.

# CHAPTER 5 BUILDING HEIGHTS AND AREAS

### 505.2 Area limitation

All mezzanines in a room, taken together, shall cover no more than one-third of that room's floor area. Within the model jurisdiction, an enclosed part of the room is counted in the room area where that enclosed part is sprinklered.

Exception: In Type I or Type II construction with an automatic sprinkler system throughout, the aggregate mezzanine area may be increased to one-half of the floor area of the room regardless of the number of storeys.

# CHAPTER 9 FIRE PROTECTION SYSTEMS

### 903.2.8 Group R

Every building that contains a Group R fire area shall be sprinklered throughout. The Model Residential Code exception is not adopted within the model jurisdiction; Group R-3 buildings with two or more dwelling units shall be sprinklered.

# CHAPTER 10 MEANS OF EGRESS

### 1004.5 Areas without fixed seating

Occupant load is computed as in Section 1004.5 of the base code, except that the occupant load factor for business areas is 100 gross square feet per occupant within the model jurisdiction.

### 1011.5 Stair treads and risers

Risers shall be no taller than 7 inches and no shorter than 4 inches. Rectangular treads shall be at least 11 inches deep. Within dwelling units in Group R-2 and R-3, the maximum riser height is 8 inches and the minimum tread depth is 9 inches. Within one flight, the tallest and shortest riser shall differ by no more than 3/8 inch.

### 1020.4 Dead ends

Where a space must have more than one exit or exit access doorway, its corridors shall be laid out so that no dead end is longer than 20 feet.

Exception: In occupancies in Groups B, M, and S where the building is equipped throughout with an automatic sprinkler system in accordance with Section 903.3.1.1, the length of the dead-end corridors shall not exceed 35 feet.
